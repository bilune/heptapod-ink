Heptapod Ink

Type an English sentence (up to 12 words). The writer turns it into a ring of ink, rereads it and fixes it until the reader understands it, and the reader then reads it from the image alone. Everything runs on your device (on the GPU if your browser supports WebGPU).

Or drop a ring image (PNG) here for the reader to read
