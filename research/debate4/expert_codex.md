**1. Expert verdict.** The interesting result is a large, visually validated encoder optimization gap in a learned discrete text codec: frozen-reader search raises fresh random exact from 12% to 54%. The 68% natural/54% random exam results cover 576 examples each, not the planned 1,000. These are supplied measurements, not independently reproduced here. I read the requested code/history, searched primary literature through November 2026, and ran only tiny CPU arithmetic checks.

A reviewer will dismiss “invented language,” prettier rings, utilization gains alone, and 95% exact on familiar short sentences. T5 already supplies language knowledge. Sender optimization has precedents in [neural compression inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04240); visual communication has [Learning to Draw](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/39d0a8908fbe6c18039ea8227f827023-Paper.pdf). However, reconstructing roughly 150 unpredictable bits is substantially harder than identifying one of ten candidate images.

My stretch target: **≥99% exact on fixed ten-word uniform combinations, ≥95% on fresh natural text, and ≥90% on both under preregistered image perturbations**, using 36×81 symbols. Additionally, a fresh reader should reach ≥90% weighted-random exact from ≤20,000 paired examples, without inherited code embeddings, adapter, or fine-tuned decoder. These are research targets, not budget forecasts. Reliable learned transmission approaching **190 payload bits/ring** (known-source entropy, never marginal code entropy), with transferable structure and substantially cheaper encoding, would interest me; 190 bits alone would not beat classical coding.

**2. The ceiling.** Nominal capacity is `36 log₂81 = 228.23` bits, or 228 freely packable binary bits. Pixel depth does not increase this deterministic alphabet. Overlap can make distinct symbol sequences visually indistinguishable.

Recomputing from `random_vocab.json`: 32,210 words, entropy **13.850 bits/word**. Ten weighted draws need 138.50 bits *on average*; ten uniform draws need 149.75. The current uniform-length 3–10 sampler averages only **93.03 bits including length**, before negligible exclusion conditioning. Report ten-word performance separately. Average entropy cannot establish a fixed-budget overflow rate; measure information-content quantiles.

For an illustrative memoryless symmetric 81-ary channel with error probability `p`, capacity per ring is `36[log₂81 − h₂(p) − p log₂80]`: **223.05 bits at 1%**, **219.02 at 1.9%**. Yet all-marks-correct probability is only **69.64%/50.13%**. These are model calculations, not measured capacity: neighboring errors, rotation, code-dependent confusion and soft evidence matter.

A constructive reference is [Reed–Solomon](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-200-principles-of-discrete-applied-mathematics-spring-2024/1aNkAeN1IETuGMFZtgBWANGHRgO76HrL4_transcript.pdf) over GF(81): `(36,32)` carries 202 binary bits before framing and corrects two symbol errors. Independent 1% errors give **≥99.44% block success**; `(36,28)` carries 177 bits and corrects four. Neither guarantee transfers from writer-conditioned mark accuracy to arbitrary codewords without measurement. Thus 139-bit random sentences are not obviously capacity-bound.

The 155/201 marginal-entropy sums upper-bound joint entropy; they are not transmitted payload. Beam collisions establish local ambiguity, not saturation. Continuous 256-dimensional success establishes learnability with a much richer channel. Present limits are quantization/task mismatch, imperfect search, shared-parameter interference, decoder priors, and possibly correlated visual errors. `MAX_T=40` truncation remains a separate correctness limit.

**3. Priority experiments.** Minutes below are H100 allocation caps, including evaluation; no jobs are launched. Every proposed local smoke is CPU-only, one thread, ≤60 seconds, synthetic/cached tiny tensors, no full-model training. Initial GPU panels use 256 paired examples per distribution; promote only on independent dev panels.

1. **Audit the channel and establish references — 20 minutes.** Compare actual per-sentence LM arithmetic coding, exact-distribution random-word coding, and the current learned codec through identical PNG/eyes. Sweep RS payloads and measure burst errors. Source coding has strong precedent in [Language Modeling Is Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10668), but its 2,048-byte chunks do not establish short-sentence bits/character. Smoke: eight framing/packing round trips and deliberately corrupted toy codewords. Pass: byte-exact source decoding, explicit overflow accounting, and batch-invariant search transforms. Otherwise stop spending on learning. In `exhaustive()`, restore the original symbol to candidate sets after edits; `alt[...,1:]` currently prevents returning to it. Key transforms by sentence/view, shared across candidates: resetting an RNG does not make differently sized batches comparable.

2. **My bet: learn a repair operator, not a replacement writer — 60 minutes.** Freeze the working codec. Train a small source-conditioned editor on search trajectories: current hard code plus source states → one `(slot,new-symbol)` action or STOP. Supervise distributions over similarly good actions, roll out on student states, and retain exact visual acceptance/fallback. This bypasses centroid-distance imitation and reduces demands on shared writer outputs. [Iterative amortized inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.09356) motivates learning updates; it does not guarantee transfer here. Allocate 20 minutes trajectories, 25 training, 15 validation. At one second/source, generation yields at most 1,200 sources; reuse their intermediate states, not imaginary large datasets. Smoke: a synthetic four-state repair task, legal actions, STOP and fallback. Pass: retain ≥90% of exhaustive search’s paired exact-match gain at ≥5× lower measured encoding time, with ≤2-point natural regression. Fail closes distillation for this budget.

3. **Demand a strong ordinary training baseline — 40 minutes.** Matched 20-minute continuations: existing STE versus [rotation gradients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06424), same batches, books initialization and noise. Rotation preserves hard forward values while changing gradients. Smoke: hard-output equality, finite zero/antipodal-vector gradients, CPU BF16 quantizer path. Pass: ≥5 random-exact points over matched continuation, ≤2 natural points lost, after PNG. [SimVQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02038) learns a shared codebook basis; [DiVeQ, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26469) changes the quantization surrogate. Both are follow-ups, not simultaneous confounds. [FSQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15505), [LFQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05737), and [BSQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07548) structure the alphabet but add no observable bits. The failed four-dimensional continuous control argues against another blind FSQ restart.

4. **Test whether anyone else can read it — 90 minutes.** Fit two independent symbolic readers; search against their mean-plus-worst loss, then freeze the sender and fit an untouched third reader. Use fresh mark embeddings/adapters and base pretrained T5, disclosing shared pretraining; reuse eyes only as a controlled symbol-recognition front end. Compare learning curves at 1k/5k/20k pairs with single-reader codes. Cap population-search generation; fill the remainder with plain codes and match that fraction across controls. [Drawing with Strangers, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10582) finds population benefits through visual grounding; arbitrary ink symbols lack that grounding, so zero-shot cross-play is not promised. Smoke: parameter-sharing and source-leakage checks with tiny mock readers. Pass: ≥10-point fresh-reader gain at 5k pairs, original accuracy within two points. Otherwise drop the transferable-language claim.

5. **Measure the rate–robustness frontier — 60 minutes.** Only after earlier gates, compare 28/32/36 active slots, forcing unused slots blank at fixed positions; adapt the winner with channel noise. Same ring/alphabet; no untransmitted residuals. [DeepJSCC-Q](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08100) supports finite-alphabet joint coding, not verbatim-text guarantees. Smoke: exact state-count accounting and deterministic mask/render checks. Pass: ≥95% ten-word random exact with 28 active slots (177.52 nominal bits), or ≥10-point perturbed gain at 36, without clean regression. Otherwise retain 36 slots; do not expand geometry merely to inflate accuracy.

**4. Evaluation an expert could accept.** Treat used Tatoeba tests and the inspected EWT exam as development evidence now. Lock new deduplicated natural documents and new-seed combinations; acknowledge unlogged historical draws and pretrained-corpus contamination. Use ≥2,000 examples each, stratified by length, rarity, capitalization, punctuation and tokenizer overflow. Include uniform ten-word combinations, weighted combinations, and withheld word-position/pair combinations. Fit vocabulary and exclusions before evaluation.

Baselines: unchanged writer, matched continuation, exhaustive search at matched wall time, receiver beam verification separately, and the non-learned codes above, plus a frozen character n-gram arithmetic coder. For natural text use a small causal LM and a pinned stronger 1–3B reference, reset context per sentence, encode EOS, termination, lengths and any escapes. Never use source-conditioned T5 likelihood as compressible bits. Pack into the same 36 symbols with/without error correction; overflow counts as failure. Report actual total-bit median/p95/p99, bits/UTF-8-byte and bits/character, and fit fraction. At 60 characters, 228 bits permits 3.80 bits/character gross; 202 permits 3.37 before framing. Short-text framing overhead must be measured; no universal English bits/character estimate is defensible here. This is an upper *performance reference*, not a theorem about optimal compression. Classical coding is evaluation-only under the trained-model constraint; report shared model storage separately. Include [L3TC (AAAI 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16642) as the efficient learned-compression reference: its RWKV design makes a compression-versus-decoding-time comparison more demanding than gzip alone.

Cross symbolic/visual, hard/soft eye outputs, 16-/8-bit PNG, clean/noisy search, original/searched codes, and single/population readers. Test independent rotation ±4°, scale ±4%, translation ±3%, noise σ=.1, plus separately reported blur/occlusion severity curves. Use deranged image controls, blank images and slot permutation. Receiver inputs must contain no source, filename, search score or candidate list from the sender.

Report cased/normalized exact, WER, block failure, latency p50/p95, compute, collisions and code/error statistics. Use paired sentence-bootstrap 95% intervals, document-clustered for natural text, Wilson intervals for exact rates, and three training seeds for the winner. Test compositionality with controlled substitutions, order reversals and causal slot edits, not topographic similarity alone; [multi-entity games](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1042/) show why feature-specific tests matter. For bit-carrying baselines report BER and block errors separately: [2026 TurboAE results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41580) illustrate that their rankings can disagree.

Actual dollar caps override minutes. Abort nonfinite loss immediately; stop a branch after two ten-minute windows with <1-point dev improvement. Require an external watchdog to terminate after 120 seconds without step progress plus a hard allocation deadline; `faulthandler` only prints traces. No large sweeps, 70B compressors, full-model local runs, further centroid-imitation variants, or OCR replacement: [Nougat](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.13418) and [DocOwl 1.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12895) solve document reading, while this system’s dominant gap is already symbolic.

**6. What would change my mind.** Strong classical baselines plus failed reader transfer would make this a compelling codec demonstration, not a new language result. Search gains disappearing after batch-invariant transforms or 8-bit rendering would invalidate the central claim. A large symbolic–visual gap would redirect effort to geometry/channel modeling. Conversely, independent-reader transfer, systematic recombination and a reproducible rate–robustness advantage at matched compute would justify a serious paper even without perfect reconstruction.
