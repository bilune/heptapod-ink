# Codex review of PLAN.md (2026-11-08)

1. **The central claim is not well posed as written.**
   - “Real language,” “not a private code,” and “circular buys something” are different claims. A private convention can be compositional; successful communication does not distinguish them.
   - Sharp replacement: **“At a fixed communication budget, a learned rotation-invariant ink protocol transmits unseen semantic compositions, is learnable by fresh receivers from limited examples, and outperforms a matched linear protocol on a prespecified robustness or sample-efficiency measure.”** Specify the meaning distribution, held-out constructions, learning budget, and minimum useful advantage.
   - Circular ≥ linear establishes, at most, noninferiority—not an advantage. Both could score zero.
   - Rotation invariance is not order independence: rings retain clockwise adjacency and can develop an internal start marker. Arbitrary permutation invariance would be a different hypothesis.
   - No finite experiment establishes “can say anything”; no failed training run disproves that some circular language could exist.

2. **Experimental-design problems**
   - **Capacity:** equal pixels, parameters, or slots do not imply equal bits. With \(L\) slots and \(K\) categorical choices, an ordered channel has at most \(L\log_2K\) bits. For independently selected ring segments and marks, \(K=R M\), including blanks. Current v2 permits about **137 nominal bits**, against only **10.34 bits** needed to identify its 1,296 meanings.
   - **Account for symmetry:** an unanchored ring identifies cyclic shifts. Count distinct rotation-equivalence classes; approximately subtract \(\log_2 L\) bits for nonperiodic sequences. Overlapping or visually indistinguishable marks reduce capacity further. Variable length also communicates information and must be charged.
   - **Use two comparisons:** same discrete inventory/length to isolate layout; then performance-versus-rate curves with physical resources matched. Match glyph resolution, ink budget, spacing, noise, occluded *signal* fraction, training budget, and tuning effort. Calibrate reliably distinguishable messages using a separate codebook task; nominal bits are not noisy-channel capacity.
   - **Architecture confound:** a sequential linear reader versus a simultaneous image reader changes more than geometry. Initially use the same image-based agents for both layouts. Test upright and randomly rotated conditions separately.
   - **Metrics:** topographic similarity measures distance preservation, not compositionality. Positional disentanglement presupposes positions that an unanchored ring lacks; bag-of-symbols disentanglement misses role binding and recursion. Add controlled mark substitutions and test whether their semantic effects transfer across contexts. [Chaabouni et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.407.pdf)
   - **Partial reveal:** measures redundancy and information placement, not advance knowledge of the sentence. Average over reveal origins and compare contiguous versus scattered masks at equal visible signal.
   - **Listener replacement:** is receiver-reset training, not by itself iterated cultural transmission. Freeze the speaker to measure acquisition curves; use limited transmitted examples and generational production if claiming iterated learning. Compare against a random codebook and a hand-designed compositional code. [Kirby, Cornish & Smith](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2504810/)
   - **Leakage:** split canonical meanings before generating images; audit equivalent meanings across splits; keep generalization tests out of tuning. Strip captions, filenames, and metadata from decipherment/photo tests. At inference, supply neither gold decoder prefixes nor target length.
   - **Thresholds:** 50% “meaning pieces” and 90% photos lack justification. Common predicates can inflate piece accuracy. Use role-aware scoring, exact semantic reconstruction, no-image/shuffled-label baselines, multiple readers, and uncertainty intervals. Twenty examples are insufficient for fine distinctions. Photo failure can reflect preprocessing/domain shift; photo success can still be a robust private barcode.

3. **Meaning source**
   - **COGS:** useful for controlled lexical/structural generalization, but itself synthetic. LF → ink → LF removes its original semantic-parsing problem. Sentence-position variable identifiers and serialization order can introduce irrelevant structure; canonicalize graphs and score modulo variable renaming/conjunct order. Audit semantic overlap and report each generalization category separately. [COGS](https://github.com/najoungkim/COGS), [ReCOGS](https://github.com/frankaging/ReCOGS)
   - **CLEVR / grounded scenes:** best for checking whether messages preserve objects, relations, and executable consequences. Use scene graphs and hard relational contrasts; successful image retrieval alone can exploit appearance. Limited abstract semantics. [CLEVR generator](https://github.com/facebookresearch/clevr-dataset-gen)
   - **SCAN:** cheap test of primitive recombination and length extrapolation, but narrow action sequences provide little evidence about general meaning. [SCAN](https://github.com/brendenlake/SCAN)
   - **AMR:** strongest listed option for broad propositional coverage, with real vocabulary, relations, and reentrancy. Annotation/parsing errors complicate interpretation, and AMR is not a complete account of meaning. [AMR guidelines](https://github.com/amrisi/amr-guidelines)
   - **Synthetic recursive grammar:** best first test of systematicity. Explicitly hold out role–filler combinations, nesting depths, and operator combinations while controlling length and lexical coverage.
   - **Sentence embeddings + retrieval:** cheapest broad-domain demonstration, weakest evidence for exact expressivity. Retrieval among \(N\) candidates requires only identifying one of \(N\) alternatives; embedding similarity may overlook negation or swapped roles.
   - **Choice:** recursive semantics first for “is it systematic”; AMR plus targeted semantic contrasts later for “how broadly can it express meaning.” None establishes unrestricted expressivity.

4. **Reuse these verified projects**
   - **[facebookresearch/EGG](https://github.com/facebookresearch/EGG):** reuse reconstruction games, discrete-channel training, population machinery, and `egg/zoo/compo_vs_generalization`. Insert your renderer between sender symbols and receiver. Audit device assumptions for MPS.
   - **[Ddaniela13/LearningToDraw](https://github.com/Ddaniela13/LearningToDraw)** — *Learning to Draw: Emergent Communication through Sketching*, Mihai & Hare: reuse visual-game design, pretrained visual backbones, and perceptual supervision. Its human interpretability depends on additional biases; it is not evidence that arbitrary ink codes become readable automatically.
   - **[frankaging/ReCOGS](https://github.com/frankaging/ReCOGS):** reuse semantic-representation diagnostics and evaluation work before implementing another LF-string benchmark.
   - **[mlfoundations/open_clip](https://github.com/mlfoundations/open_clip):** use a frozen image encoder with a small trained receiver head, or frozen scene/text encoders for a grounded retrieval baseline. Keep a small CNN control: natural-image pretraining may discard ink distinctions.
   - **[huggingface/sentence-transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/sentence-transformers):** cache pretrained sentence embeddings as speaker inputs for a broad retrieval pilot. This imports semantic structure; do not describe it as semantics emerging from scratch.
   - These provide reusable components, not an existing implementation of the proposed circular-language claim.

5. **Cheapest useful falsification experiment**
   - **Scope:** falsify the practical claim that this implementation learns a transferable systematic protocol—not the existential possibility of circular language.
   - Keep the existing toy meanings initially. Replace random triple holdout with an entity excluded from the subject role but observed as object, plus held-out subject–object combinations. Include role-reversal minimal pairs.
   - Compare ring and line using a small, visually distinct shared alphabet, a fixed short message budget, the same CNN receiver, hard discrete evaluation, and three paired seeds. Include an engineered compositional code as a positive control and a no-image baseline.
   - Train for roughly 2,000–3,000 steps per condition; batch 64, existing 96-pixel rendering, MPS. Freeze speakers and train fresh receivers on increasing example budgets. Evaluate semantic exact match, role errors, and rotation robustness.
   - **Decisive negative:** strong training accuracy but poor held-out role binding or fresh-receiver transfer, while the engineered control succeeds. A reproducible linear advantage rejects circular superiority under these conditions. If every condition fails, the setup is inconclusive.
   - Six small runs plus receiver probes plausibly fit under a day based on the README timings; that is an estimate, not a measured guarantee. Benchmark one run and impose a fixed compute cap. Defer COGS, foundation-model training, humans, and printing.

6. **Concrete v1/v2 issues**
   - **Stochastic topographic evaluation:** [`evaluate()`](../v1/heptapod.py) unconditionally restores training mode; the subsequent topographic function never calls `eval()`. Consequently, v2 samples Gumbel messages for that metric rather than measuring its deterministic deployed code. Reported reconstruction accuracy is evaluated in the correct mode.
   - **Incorrect Spearman implementation:** [`argsort().argsort()` plus random jitter](../v1/heptapod.py) does not handle the many tied semantic distances correctly. Use average ranks and repeated meaning-label permutations; also use rotation-invariant message distances.
   - **Continuous shortcuts remain in v1:** pixel noise does not discretize a channel. Width, radial offset, splash amount, and length remain continuous; the decisiveness penalty targets only ink amplitude.
   - **Displayed images are not tested images:** [`paint()`](../v2/heptapod_ink.py) changes resolution and contrast; `say()` decodes the separate low-resolution rendering. Existing accuracy therefore does not validate exported or printed glyphs.
   - **Unfair holistic baseline:** [`HolisticSpeaker` training](../v1/heptapod.py) sees every meaning, uses a lookup table, and receives different optimization. It cannot assess unseen-meaning generalization.
   - **Reproducibility:** v2 seeds Torch but not Python’s RNG used for occlusion. Its checkpoint omits the run configuration and external-library provenance.
   - **Results/configuration drift:** the **93.87%** result is in [`out_run3/metrics.json`](../v2/out_run3/metrics.json), with 40 ring segments. Current code selects 32; [`out/metrics.json`](../v2/out/metrics.json) is a 30-step run with **0% held-out exact accuracy**. Preserve matching code, library, split, configuration, and checkpoint before treating that result as reproducible.
